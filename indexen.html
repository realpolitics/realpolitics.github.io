Currency:
Money is a unit for facilitating the exchange of goods and services within a society and for creating life in a given area.
The value of money is not correlated with its quantity but with the effectiveness of its circulation in covering personal needs and creating value for export.
The state is not a business, but there must be businesses within the state, and it is their condition that creates the value of the currency.

Society:
Raising social and environmental standards to the highest possible will completely change the attitude and standard of living of the entire society.
This will facilitate the creation of a true, highly developed civilization, where everything is possible, and everyone can pursue their passions.
The system is merely a set of rules meant to enable and facilitate further development and existence in the world, at a level we choose ourselves.

Financial System:
Changes in the financial system are necessary and crucial for creating the proper form of the social system.
Basic personal income constitutes the first of many important steps that society will feel.
A fully functional monetary system fulfills its theoretical assumptions, becoming a tool for exchanging goods and services within society and creating life in a given area.
The government serves as the issuer and distributor of money in such a way as to effectively manage material, environmental, and social resources in the long term.
These changes bring benefits to all social groups, both producers and consumers of goods and services.

Taxes:
The government serves as the issuer and distributor of money, eliminating the need for any taxes.
This significantly simplifies the relationships between economic entities and society with the government.
At the same time, it allows the government to focus on its proper function, which is directing proper development and raising living standards,
so that society can develop and exist in the best possible form, while also ensuring safety and environmental protection.
The government issues an income supplement, which serves to catalog the supply and demand of goods and services for market purposes and to support emerging directions.
The income supplement is only granted on earned income from registered entities.

Safety:
A well-designed social system eliminates over 90% of crime, significantly increasing the sense of security.
Appropriate laws and a high level of services ensure 100% safety.

Possibilities:
The government, unrestricted in its actions, can create and support new directions in all fields, contributing to the emergence of many socially and economically useful places.
More time and greater freedom in a safe and friendly environment create many opportunities for both the individual and the entire society, contributing to the development of both the individual and society as a whole.
Basic structures of the state's existence as a whole must be owned by the state and provided free of charge to citizens.
Structures: social housing, public transportation, legal protection, healthcare.
Utilities: energy, connectivity, water, municipal services including waste (at the basic level of average household consumption relative to structure efficiency).
Such a solution minimizes the number of unnecessary tasks, increasing efficiency through automation and globalization.

Globalization:
State globalization, meaning the development and establishment of state-owned companies to fully meet all the needs of the state and create value for export.
The end product, as the highest-profitable outcome, will enable favorable exchange with other countries, increasing import and production possibilities.
Long-term, proper management of natural resources is a significant factor in minimizing negative market effects, such as
creating unnecessary jobs, products of reduced quality and durability, waste production, environmental degradation, or market shortages.
Higher quality standards positively impact health by reducing negative effects
resulting from both environmental degradation and product quality, food, and lifestyle standards.

Basic Income:
Basic income - upon reaching adulthood, everyone receives unconditional personal income in an amount sufficient to maintain the current level of the social minimum,
increased as automation and globalization progress in the country, thus reducing the amount of work necessary to cover all market and social demands.
Basic income for individuals in the 0-18 age range is transferred to the parents' or legal guardians' account. Such a solution increases the standard of living in society,
stabilizes the market, and enhances security by eliminating the basic causes of social, economic, and environmental crime development.

Income/Earnings:
Income/Earnings - The full income is calculated from 3 basic values:
1 The first value of the product/service is calculated from the cost-profit ratio based on current principles, reduced by the disappearing taxation.
And two additional values added by the Government to the basic settlement of the current accounting period.
2 The second value added is the income supplement, which is a tool for cataloging market supply
and calculated based on the ratio of supply of performed work (read: earnings) to the number of people receiving
basic personal income from the current accounting period.
This value is remuneration for work and contribution to society and reflects the social usefulness of the product/service.
This value may be suspended as a penalty in case of violation of economic, environmental, and social regulations by the entity.
3 The third value is internally calculated by government bodies based on needs and quality assessment.
The basic value is 0 and is arbitrarily and unrestrictedly granted by government bodies to selected entities based on the assessment of appropriate offices.
This is a tool for controlling supply relative to demand for goods and services. Such a solution helps control economic development in the country to fully meet all state needs.

The application of progressive settlement of the income supplement preserves all the essential characteristics of a free market.
It supports emerging directions and entities, encourages work, while maintaining the appropriate correlation of costs, product/service values, and money in the market relative to the size of the population.

ACTIVE DEMOCRACY:
Active democracy is the only democratic system that gives society real influence over the quality of governance and the selection and dismissal of appointed officials at any time without a specified election term.
This is done by collecting votes of dissent continuously both online and at designated offices for the corresponding area.
Upon reaching a specified threshold of dissatisfaction for the corresponding area, new elections are announced
during which dissenting voices are publicly considered, as well as new candidacies.
The percentage value is calculated for each type of position as a percentage of the eligible voting population in the respective area.
For county-level functions from the entire county and national functions from the entire country. Upon reaching a specified threshold of 30%, an analysis process begins where the current candidate
can appropriately explain their decisions and present the further course of their candidacy, and the process of new candidate campaigns begins.
The election date is set within a period not exceeding six months after reaching the designated threshold.
The greatest advantages of this solution are:
Direct influence of society on the appointment of official positions (read: true democracy)
The ability to focus current officials solely on the tasks required by their function without unnecessary interruptions for campaigning when it is not needed.
Fully transparent and clear elections both electronically and at designated offices for the corresponding area.
